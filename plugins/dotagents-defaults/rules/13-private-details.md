## Private Details

Keep private details out of public places. Public repositories, and anything posted to them, must not reveal private infrastructure or mix separate parts of my life.

- Don't name or link private hosts, networks, internal domains, addresses, or repositories on private servers. This covers public repositories' files, commit messages, branch names, pull request descriptions, issues, and comments.
- Keep personal and work contexts apart. Don't bring work projects, employers, or colleagues into personal public repositories, or personal projects and details into work ones.
- Anything committed stays in history, even after a later commit deletes it. That includes plan files and other temporary files.
- When a public change has to refer to something private, describe it generically, such as "a separate repository" or "a self-hosted runner".
- If a private detail lands somewhere public, fix the visible text right away and tell me. Removing it from pushed history needs a force push, so ask first.

Local plugins may name specific private hosts and projects to watch for.
