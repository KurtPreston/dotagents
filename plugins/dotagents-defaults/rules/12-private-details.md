## Private Details

Public repositories and posts should reveal nothing that isn't already public. That covers personal and work alike: private repositories, networks, hosts and services, and what's installed on my machines.

- In public files, commits, and pull requests, refer to non-public things generically, such as "a separate repository" or "a self-hosted runner", rather than by name.
- Deleted files stay in history, so keep non-public details out of temporary files too.

Local plugins may list specific names to watch for.
